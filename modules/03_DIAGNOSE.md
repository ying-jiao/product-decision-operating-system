# Module 3 — Diagnose

## Purpose

Convert approved evidence into the most defensible view of the product problem, constraint, opportunity, bottleneck, or strategic tension. Preserve credible competing explanations.

DIAGNOSE answers: **What do we need to solve?**

## Inputs

- latest approved FRAME and ANALYZE;
- relevant structured data required to verify approved findings.

Missing data is not evidence.

## Method

1. Restate the outcome being constrained or the decision being faced.
2. Locate the observed problem area without claiming a cause.
3. Generate two to four genuinely different explanations when evidence permits.
4. For each hypothesis, capture supporting evidence, weakening evidence, missing evidence, and a prediction.
5. Ask what else could create the same observed pattern.
6. Select one leading diagnosis when evidence supports it. Use `root cause` only when causality is established.
7. Rate confidence High, Medium, or Low and explain why; do not invent probabilities.
8. Steelman the strongest competing explanation and state what would make it become the leader.
9. Define an observable prediction and a diagnosis-level falsifier.
10. If a missing fact makes action irresponsible, request the minimum evidence needed; otherwise proceed conditionally.

[...]

## Executive view

Show the leading diagnosis, why it leads, strongest competitor, calibrated confidence, falsifier, implication for SOLUTIONS + DECIDE, and judgments requiring review.

## Quality gate

- Does the diagnosis follow from approved evidence?
- Are competing hypotheses meaningfully different?
- Is contradictory evidence visible?
- Is correlation being overstated as causation?
- Is confidence calibrated?
- Are prediction and falsifier observable?
- Has solution generation been excluded?
