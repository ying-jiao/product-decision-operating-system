# Module 4 — Solutions + Decide

## Purpose

Generate the smallest useful set of credible approaches at the right altitude, compare them fairly, and choose one primary first bet.

SOLUTIONS + DECIDE answers: **What should we do?**

## Inputs

- latest approved FRAME, ANALYZE, and DIAGNOSE;
- relevant structured evidence needed to evaluate alternatives.

## Method

1. Confirm the objective, leading diagnosis, constraints, and important uncertainty.
2. Choose the solution altitude required by the brief: strategic model, product approach, operating-model change, or another real decision level.
3. Generate roughly two to four materially different approaches. Do not add filler or split cosmetic variants.
4. For each approach, state what it is, how it addresses the diagnosis, theory of change, upside, downside, dependency, and unique organizational leverage.
5. If AI or another technology is proposed, identify its actual leverage; never use technology as a strategy label.
6. Check overlap: choosing one option should allocate meaningful resources differently from another.
7. Compare on decision-specific criteria that genuinely differentiate the options. Avoid double counting and fake precision.
8. Select one first bet. State why it addresses the diagnosis, advances the objective, and should come first.
9. Steelman the strongest alternative, including what it does better.
10. State the explicit trade-off, what is not being pursued, and what new evidence would reverse the choice.

## Quality gate

- Is the solution altitude correct?
- Are the options materially different and diagnosis-linked?
- Are the criteria decision-specific and non-overlapping?
- Is there one clear first bet?
- Is the runner-up genuinely credible?
- Does the trade-off sacrifice something valuable?
- Is uncertainty visible without avoiding the decision?
- Has implementation detail been left downstream?
